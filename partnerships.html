Our Alliance

Who We Partner With

Forging meaningful alliances across sectors to drive holistic, sustainable and systemic transformation — every sector has a role in a wellbeing Bangladesh.

  1. Governments

    Public institutions and ministries embedding wellbeing in policy, planning and public programmes.

  2. Educational Institutions

    Schools, colleges and universities advancing wellbeing on campus and in the classroom.

  3. Healthcare Organisations

    Preventive care, mental health and community health providers placing wellbeing before illness.

  4. Corporate & CSR Partners

    Businesses investing in workplace wellbeing and community impact where they operate.

  5. NGOs & Civil Society

    Local organisations delivering programmes where people live, learn and work.

  6. Research Institutions

    Universities and think tanks co-developing evidence for the Bangladesh Wellbeing Framework™.

  7. Environmental Organisations

    Partners protecting the natural systems that wellbeing ultimately depends on.

  8. Community Leaders

    Grassroots champions who mobilise their neighbours and hold the trust of their communities.

  9. Philanthropic Foundations

    Funders backing long-term, generational impact instead of quick wins.

How We Work

Partnerships Built on Trust

We begin every collaboration with a shared question — what will change for people if we work together? From that answer we design programmes with clear roles, honest measurement and room for local ownership.

Partners bring unique knowledge, reach and strengths; we bring the framework, the programme design and a decade-long commitment to the mission. Together we pilot, learn and scale what works.

Whether you represent a ministry, a company, a campus or a community group, there is a place for you in the wellbeing movement.

How We Show Up

Partnership Principles

Five commitments that guide every memorandum, every programme and every decision we make together.

  1. 01

    Shared Purpose

    We begin with a common vision and shared values, ensuring alignment at every level of the partnership.

  2. 02

    Mutual Respect

    Every partner contributes unique knowledge, experience and strengths to the ecosystem — none is an afterthought.

  3. 03

    Integrity

    Transparency, ethics and accountability guide every collaboration and every decision made in it.

  4. 04

    Long-Term Value

    We prioritise relationships that create sustainable, generational impact over quick, visible wins.

  5. 05

    Collective Responsibility

    Success and responsibility are shared, fostering a genuine sense of community ownership over outcomes.
