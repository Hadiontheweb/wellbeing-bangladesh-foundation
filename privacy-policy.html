Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026. Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates this website (the “Site”). This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to share with us, plus basic technical data needed to run the Site:

  • Name, email address and phone number when you fill out a contact or enquiry form.
  • The content of your message and the topic you select, so we can route it to the right team.
  • Basic technical data such as pages visited, time on site and general location derived from your IP address, where analytics tools are active.
  • Cookies used for essential site functionality and, where applicable, to understand how the Site is used.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and keep you updated on your request.
  • Send programme updates or newsletters you have explicitly requested.
  • Improve the content, structure and performance of the Site.
  • Protect the Site and its visitors from abuse and misuse.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

We share limited information with the services needed to operate the Site — our hosting provider, email delivery and analytics. Some pages display images and fonts served by third-party content networks (such as Unsplash and Google Fonts), which may receive basic request data such as your IP address. These services process data under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as long as required by law. When it is no longer needed, we delete it safely.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@wellbeingbangladesh.org.

6. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help the Site function and help us understand what is useful. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may affect how some parts of the Site work.

7. Children's Privacy

Much of our work involves children and young people, but the Site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Where a programme involves participants under 18, we work through schools, institutions and guardians and collect only what the programme requires.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes are posted on this page with an updated date, so we encourage you to review it occasionally.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Write to us at Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation, Dhaka, Bangladesh, or email info@wellbeingbangladesh.org.

For Everyone

Accessibility Statement

Wellbeing means everyone. We are committed to making this website usable by the widest possible audience, regardless of ability or technology.

Our Commitment

We aim to meet modern accessibility guidance (including WCAG 2.1 Level AA) and to improve continuously as the Site evolves.

What We Do

  • Semantic page structure and meaningful headings for screen readers.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images throughout the Site.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, sliders and interactive elements.
  • Colour contrast checked for text and controls.
  • Responsive layouts that work from small phones to large desktop screens.
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences in animations.

Known Limitations

Some older archived content may not yet fully meet these standards. We are working through it and welcome reports.

Need Help or Want to Report a Barrier?

If any part of this Site is difficult to use, tell us — we will fix it where we can and offer the information another way. Email info@wellbeingbangladesh.org or use our contact form.
