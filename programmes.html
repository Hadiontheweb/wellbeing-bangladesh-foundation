Transforming Knowledge into Practice. Practice into Purpose.

At Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation, learning is not confined to classrooms, books or theories. Our programmes are designed to be lived, practised and shared — creating change that lasts.

How We Learn

Practice into Purpose. Purpose into Impact.

“Knowledge grows when it is shared. Practice deepens when it is guided. Impact multiplies when it is together.” — Our Learning Philosophy

What We Offer

Core Programme Areas

  1. School Wellbeing

    Healthy School Initiative · student wellbeing sessions · teacher training · play-based learning · parent engagement across partner schools.

  2. Campus & Youth

    Healthy Campus Initiative · peer-support networks · mental health awareness · Youth Leadership Academy · volunteer opportunities.

  3. Workplace Wellbeing

    Healthy Workplace Bangladesh · leadership workshops · stress and resilience training · people-first policy support for employers.

  4. Family & Parenting

    Family Wellbeing Initiative · parenting circles · communication workshops · support for caregivers across every stage of family life.

  5. Women's Wellbeing

    Women Wellness Initiative · health education · maternal wellbeing · skill building · community support circles for women.

  6. Community Programmes

    Community Wellness Initiative · community champions · wellbeing camps · grassroots events led by local volunteers.

How It Works

One Journey, Four Stages

Learn

Every programme begins with practical, evidence-informed learning — delivered in plain language, rooted in Bangladeshi culture and designed for real life, not theory.

WorkshopsToolkitsGroup SessionsBangla & English

Practice

Participants practise new skills with guided support — at school, at work and at home — until wellbeing becomes habit, not homework.

Daily RitualsPeer GroupsHabit ToolsOngoing Coaching

Lead

Graduates step up as wellbeing champions — leading sessions, supporting peers and becoming the local heart of every programme.

Champion TrainingMentorshipCommunity SessionsRecognition

Multiply

Champions train others, schools mentor schools, workplaces inspire workplaces — impact multiplies across the ecosystem, sustainably and at scale.

Train-the-TrainerPartner NetworkOpen ResourcesNational Movement
