Home/Programmes

Transforming Knowledge into Practice. Practice into Purpose.

At Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation, learning is not confined to classrooms, books or theories. Our programmes are designed to be lived, practised and shared — creating change that lasts.

What We Offer

Core Programme Areas

Preventive Wellness

Staying well before illness begins — nutrition, movement, sleep, mental health awareness and healthy habits taught in schools, homes and communities.

School Wellbeing

Healthy School Initiative · wellbeing sessions · teacher training · play-based learning and parent engagement across partner schools.

Campus & Youth

Healthy Campus Initiative · peer-support networks · mental health awareness and the Youth Leadership Academy for young changemakers.

Workplace Wellbeing

Healthy Workplace Bangladesh · leadership workshops · stress and resilience training · people-first policy support for employers.

Family & Parenting

Family Wellbeing Initiative · parenting circles · communication workshops and support for caregivers across every stage of family life.

Women's Wellbeing

Women Wellness Initiative · health education · maternal wellbeing · skill building and community support circles for women.

Community Programmes

Community Wellness Initiative · community champions · wellbeing camps and grassroots events led by local volunteers.

Nature-Based Learning

Outdoor classrooms, environmental stewardship and climate-aware living — reconnecting young people with the land that sustains them.

Wisdom Education

Timeless values meet modern science — critical thinking, ethics and conscious decision-making for lifelong growth.

Professional Development

Training & Certification

“Our goal is not simply to certify individuals, but to develop responsible practitioners who embody the values and philosophy of the Foundation.”

We offer structured training pathways for educators, health workers, HR professionals, youth leaders and volunteers — combining theory, supervised practice and community service.
