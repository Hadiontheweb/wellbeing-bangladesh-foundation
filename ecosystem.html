One Vision. Multiple Initiatives. Shared Purpose.

Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation is more than a single organisation — it is a growing ecosystem of programmes, partners, institutions and communities working toward one shared national goal.

The Foundation

The Parent Institution

Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation is the foundation of the entire ecosystem. It provides vision, governance, philosophy and leadership — together with institutional systems, strategic direction and quality assurance across every initiative.

From this foundation, specialised programmes grow — each focused on a distinct part of the national wellbeing mission, each aligned to the Bangladesh Wellbeing Framework™.

A Growing Family of Initiatives

Healthy School Initiative

Creating healthier schools where children learn, play and grow in nurturing environments. We partner with schools across Bangladesh to embed wellbeing into daily school life — from mindful mornings to teacher training and family engagement.

Wellbeing CurriculumTeacher TrainingPlay & MovementParent Workshops

Empowering university students with wellbeing skills, peer support networks and healthy campus cultures — preparing the next generation to lead lives of purpose, balance and contribution.

Transforming workplaces into spaces of productivity, balance and professional wellbeing. We work with employers to build people-first cultures where teams and businesses thrive together.

Our Approach

Why an Ecosystem?

  1. 01

    Specialisation

    Each initiative focuses on a specific domain — allowing depth, quality and cultural fit in every setting we serve.

  2. 02

    Shared Standards

    Every initiative follows the Framework™, shared training and common measurement — so quality never depends on place.

  3. 03

    Collaboration

    Initiatives refer, learn and build on one another — a school connects to a campus, a campus to workplaces, workplaces to communities.

  4. 04

    Scale with Soul

    The ecosystem grows through partners and local leaders — reaching more of Bangladesh without losing the human touch.
