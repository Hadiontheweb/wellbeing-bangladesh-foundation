Who We Are

A National Movement for Wellbeing, Human Development and Social Impact.

Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation is a non-profit, non-political and impact-driven organization committed to advancing holistic wellbeing for individuals, families, workplaces and communities across Bangladesh.

We believe that lasting national progress begins with people who are healthy, resilient, empowered and purpose-driven. Our work brings together education, research, innovation, leadership development and community engagement to improve quality of life and create sustainable social impact.

Rather than addressing individual challenges in isolation, we adopt a holistic approach that recognizes the interconnected nature of physical health, mental wellbeing, family relationships, education, professional growth, financial resilience, ethical values, cultural identity and environmental sustainability.

Through strategic partnerships with government institutions, educational organizations, healthcare providers, businesses, development partners and local communities, we strive to build a Bangladesh where wellbeing becomes a shared national priority and every person has the opportunity to thrive.

Why Wellbeing Matters

Because Sustainable Development Begins with Human Wellbeing.

The world is changing rapidly. While nations continue to make progress in technology, infrastructure and economic growth, many people still struggle with stress, loneliness, unhealthy lifestyles, family challenges, financial insecurity, environmental risks and a lack of purpose.

True progress cannot be measured by economic indicators alone. A nation's greatest strength lies in the health, resilience and wellbeing of its people.

At Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation, we believe that wellbeing is the cornerstone of sustainable development. When individuals are physically healthy, mentally resilient, emotionally balanced and socially connected, they become more productive, compassionate and capable of contributing to their families, workplaces and communities.

Investing in wellbeing is not simply an investment in individuals — it is an investment in stronger families, healthier communities and a more prosperous Bangladesh.

We believe...

  • Healthy PeoplebuildStrong Families.
  • Strong FamiliesbuildResilient Communities.
  • Resilient Communitiesbuilda Prosperous Nation.

Why Bangladesh Needs a Wellbeing Movement

Bangladesh has made remarkable progress in many areas of development. The next stage of national progress requires a greater focus on human wellbeing — supporting healthier lifestyles, stronger mental health, empowered youth, resilient families, productive workplaces, ethical leadership and sustainable communities.

By placing wellbeing at the centre of development, we can create lasting social impact that benefits present and future generations.

Wellbeing is not the outcome of development. It is the foundation of development.

Our Signature Model

The Bangladesh Wellbeing Framework™

A Holistic Framework for Human Flourishing and Sustainable National Development

At Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation, we believe that true wellbeing goes beyond the absence of illness. It is the presence of health, purpose, resilience, opportunity and meaningful human connection.

The Bangladesh Wellbeing Framework™ is our signature model for understanding and advancing holistic wellbeing. It recognizes that every dimension of life is interconnected, and that lasting human development can only be achieved when individuals, families and communities flourish together.

This framework provides a practical roadmap for individuals, institutions and communities to create healthier lives, stronger relationships, meaningful work and sustainable social impact.

“Wellbeing is achieved when every dimension of life grows in harmony.”
