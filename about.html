Our Story

Every Great Movement Begins with a Simple Belief.

Bangladesh has made remarkable progress in many areas of development. Yet millions of people continue to face challenges that affect their overall wellbeing.

We believe the future of Bangladesh depends not only on economic growth, but on healthier people, stronger families and more resilient communities.

This belief inspired the establishment of Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation — a national movement dedicated to advancing holistic wellbeing and creating lasting social impact.

Who We Are

A National Movement for Wellbeing, Human Development and Social Impact

Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation is a non-profit, non-political and impact-driven organization committed to improving the wellbeing of individuals, families, workplaces and communities.

Guided by the Bangladesh Wellbeing Framework™, we work through education, research, innovation, leadership development and strategic partnerships to create healthier lives and stronger communities.

Why We Exist

Because Wellbeing is the Foundation of Sustainable Development.

Lasting national progress begins with healthy, empowered and resilient people. We exist to place wellbeing at the heart of development and help create a future where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

Our Vision

A Wellbeing Bangladesh where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

We envision a future where every person, every family and every community enjoys better health, greater resilience and an improved quality of life.

Our Mission

To empower people, strengthen families and build resilient communities through holistic wellbeing.

We achieve this through education, research, innovation, advocacy and strategic partnerships that create measurable and sustainable social impact.

Our Wellbeing Philosophy

Wellbeing First. People Always.

We believe sustainable development begins with human wellbeing. When people thrive, families grow stronger. When families flourish, communities become more resilient. Together, they create a healthier, happier and more sustainable nation.
