The Purpose of Our Institution

Why do we exist?

To serve the people of Bangladesh through holistic wellbeing — placing health, dignity, resilience and opportunity at the heart of national development.

Progress is not measured by growth alone. A truly developed Bangladesh is one where every person can thrive — physically, mentally, socially and economically — across generations.

What does the nation need?

A decisive shift from reactive cure to proactive prevention — addressing the root causes of health disparities, emotional distress and social vulnerability.

Practical, human-centered systems that reach families, schools and youth where they live, cultivating daily wellbeing habits and resilient community safety nets.

What are we uniquely positioned to contribute?

An integrated national ecosystem uniting scientific evidence, grassroots wisdom, transformative education and multi-sector partnerships.

A holistic framework tailored to Bangladesh’s unique cultural landscape, bridging policy, communities and individuals into one unified movement for wellbeing.

How do we sustain our mission?

Through transparent governance, empirical research, strategic coalitions and enduring institutional stewardship.

By empowering individuals, schools and workplaces to become self-sustaining hubs of wellbeing, ensuring our collective impact deepens for generations to come.

What We Work Toward

Four Aims of a Wellbeing Bangladesh

  1. Healthy People

    Every person enjoys better physical, mental and emotional health — with prevention placed before cure.

  2. Strong Families

    Families are supported as the first school of wellbeing — connected, resilient and nurturing.

  3. Resilient Communities

    Communities hold the tools, confidence and leadership to shape their own wellbeing journeys.

  4. Sustainable Nation

    Wellbeing becomes a shared national priority — embedded in policy, workplaces, schools and culture.

The Whole Person

Six Dimensions of Wellbeing

Every challenge a person, family or community faces can be understood through core interconnected dimensions. The Framework™ treats them as one integrated whole — because wellbeing is indivisible.

  1. 01

    Physical Wellbeing

    Nutrition, movement, sleep, and preventive healthcare for lasting vitality, physical strength, and proactive wellness.

  2. 02

    Mental & Emotional

    Psychological resilience, stress regulation, emotional balance, mindfulness, and proactive mental health support.

  3. 03

    Social & Family

    Nurturing family bonds, positive relationships, social connection, empathy, and resilient community cohesion.

  4. 04

    Financial Resilience

    Financial literacy, disciplined savings habits, productive livelihoods, and sustainable economic security.

  5. 05

    Purpose & Values

    Inner meaning, personal fulfillment, ethical grounding, mindfulness, and conscious, responsible citizenship.

  6. 06

    Environmental & Community

    Clean living environments, climate awareness, sustainable lifestyles, and collective community stewardship.
