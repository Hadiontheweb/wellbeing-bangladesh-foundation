Building a Legacy for Future Generations

Our vision is a Bangladesh where wellbeing is a shared national priority — where every person, in every generation, has the opportunity to thrive.

The Long View

A Vision in Three Eras

National wellbeing is not built in a single campaign. Our vision unfolds across three eras — each one laying the ground for the next.

01

Foundation

Establishing the Bangladesh Wellbeing Framework™, the institution and the flagship programmes that anchor the movement.

02

Expansion

Taking proven programmes to every district — schools, campuses, workplaces and communities nationwide.

03

Legacy

Embedding wellbeing into national culture, policy and education, so future generations inherit the gains.

Where We Focus

Strategic Priorities

Eight priorities turn a long-term vision into yearly action — each mapped to the ten dimensions of the Bangladesh Wellbeing Framework™.

  1. 01

    Holistic Wellbeing

    Advancing the ten dimensions of wellbeing as one integrated national agenda, not separate agendas.

  2. 02

    Preventive Health

    Placing prevention before cure — healthy lifestyles, mental resilience and early support for every family.

  3. 03

    Education & Learning

    Weaving wellbeing into curricula, teacher training and lifelong learning from primary school onward.

  4. 04

    Strong Families

    Supporting families as the first school of wellbeing — connected, resilient and nurturing across generations.

  5. 05

    Ethical Leadership

    Growing purpose-driven leaders who serve people before positions, in every sector of society.

  6. 06

    Economic Resilience

    Building financial capability, dignified work and real opportunity so no family is one shock from crisis.

  7. 07

    Sustainable Communities

    Mobilising communities to lead their own wellbeing journeys in balance with the natural world.

  8. 08

    Research & Innovation

    Generating and applying evidence that keeps the movement honest, adaptive and effective.
