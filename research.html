Advancing Knowledge. Inspiring Innovation. Creating Lasting Impact.

How can knowledge improve quality of life? This question guides every research initiative, educational programme and publication produced by Wellbeing Bangladesh Foundation.

Our Approach

Research Rooted in Real Life

Guided by curiosity, integrity and interdisciplinary collaboration, our inquiries break down traditional silos to understand the connections between individual wellbeing, family life, community strength and national progress.

From field studies in rural districts to campus-level pilots and workplace surveys, every question we ask begins with a simple test — will this knowledge help people live healthier, more purposeful lives? Evidence gathered in the field flows straight back into the programmes communities rely on.

We publish what we learn, share it freely and invite institutions, partners and students to build on it alongside us.

Where We Enquire

Areas of Research

Our inquiries follow the ten dimensions of the Bangladesh Wellbeing Framework™ — understanding wellbeing as one connected system, not a set of separate problems.

  1. 01

    Holistic Wellbeing Science

    Physical, mental, social and economic dimensions studied as one connected system — measuring how progress in one area of life shapes all the others.

  2. 02

    Human Development & Education

    How learning, life skills and leadership development shape wellbeing across childhood, youth and adulthood — in schools, campuses and beyond.

  3. 03

    Community & Social Impact

    What makes communities resilient — and how volunteerism, local institutions and social trust translate into measurable, lasting change.

From Research to Practice

How Knowledge Moves

Findings only matter when they travel. We turn evidence into publications, conversations and tools that educators, partners and communities can use immediately.

  1. Publications & Reports

    Research briefs, white papers and annual reports from the Bangladesh Wellbeing Framework™ — written to be read, not filed away.

  2. Workshops & Seminars

    Conversations that put findings in front of educators, practitioners, policymakers and students across the country.

  3. Knowledge Resources

    Toolkits, frameworks and reading lists that help institutions apply wellbeing research in their own settings.

  4. Digital Learning

    Articles, videos and online sessions that carry the movement far beyond the rooms where research begins.
